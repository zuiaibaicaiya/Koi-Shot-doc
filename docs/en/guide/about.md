# About Us

koiShot is a small capture tool for Windows, macOS and Linux: capture and annotate, pin an image next to your work, stitch long pages, record GIFs or MP4, copy text out of pictures and turn tables into Excel. Recognition runs on your machine — no sign-in, no ads, no watermarks.

## Updates & Open Source

Updates happen automatically: Windows installs and reopens the app on its own, while macOS / Linux open the downloaded package for you to finish in the system dialog. To check progress or retry, look at the status line in Settings → About (see [Settings & Tray](/en/guide/settings)); you can also install a newer package yourself (see [Get Started](/en/guide/start/getting-started)).

To see the open-source components and their licenses, open Settings → About → Open-source licenses.

## Reporting Issues

When reporting a problem in the group, these help us reply faster:

1. A screenshot or recording of the problem;
2. Your OS (Windows / macOS / Linux) and koiShot version;
3. The steps to reproduce it.
